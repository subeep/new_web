Active detection

DroneDOOT™

3D Active Radar-Based Drone Detector

  • 5 kminstrumented range
  • 5 kmclassification
  • 360°azimuth
Details

A 3D track-and-scan X-band radar providing pinpoint precision in detection of aerial targets. Based on micro-Doppler technology, its performance is enhanced by a backend AI engine that classifies different target types. DroneDOOT™ can be interfaced with any weapon system and other dual-use platforms.

Passive detection

DroneSENSE™

Wide-Band RF-Based Passive Drone Detector

  • MLIEmulti-layer inference
  • ~0intercept probability
  • Anymake / model
Details

Leverages radio signals emitted between drones and remote controllers for telemetry and video streaming. Multi-layer inference engines (MLIE) detect the presence of drones in any given area. Being passive, it achieves negligible probability of intercept, and it is protocol-agnostic — detecting any make or model. Integrates with multiple dual-use platforms.

Soft-kill EW

DroneYUG™

Soft-Kill Drone Countermeasure System

  • FHSSproprietary jamming
  • GPSintegrated spoofer
  • Safeguided landing
Details

An electronic warfare system that counters drone threats by orchestrating jamming and spoofing. Its proprietary jamming mechanism tackles FHSS drones at long range, while an integrated GPS spoofing unit lets operators manoeuvre the drone to a safe landing for forensic analysis. Supports a wake-and-sleep model and integrates with RF / EO-IR / camera-based detection.

Handheld

DroneARM™

Handheld Drone Jammer Gun

  • Longrange defeat
  • 2-handrifle-style design
  • RTHland / return home
Details

A lightweight, robust drone countermeasure gun with high-performance directional antennas and an intuitive control panel to select and engage jamming frequencies. When disruption is triggered, targets respond via vertical on-the-spot landing or by returning to their home, remote controller or starting point — a safe option against a wide range of drone threats.

All-band

DroneWALL™ / DIWAR™

Parametric Drone Jammer

  • 433MHz
  • 900MHz
  • GNSS 
  • 2.4GHz
  • 5.8GHz
Details

An all-band jammer for tackling FHSS (Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum) drones and inhibiting their motion over the air. A modular, low-power device that acts as an intrusion wall and repulsion device for neutralising FHSS drones across diverse scenarios.

Friend or foe

DroneDRISHTI™

Remote ID Broadcaster for IFF Drones

  • GPScoordinates
  • IMUaccel + gyro
  • Ultralightweight
Details

A drone-mountable Identification of Friend or Foe (IFF) remote ID module broadcasting location coordinates, accelerometer and gyroscopic data. Integrates with Avgarde’s detection systems to separate white-listed from black-listed drones.
