About Avgarde

Risk mitigation for the low-altitude airspace.

AVGARDE® is a radio frequency, radar sensing and wireless technology deeptech startup building an AI-enabled situational awareness and aerial object sensing platform for risk mitigation and management of the low-altitude airspace.

DroneAstra™ addresses the challenges drones present to critical infrastructure, military installations, civil aviation, drone operators and the general public — across airports, industrial security, fleet operations, homeland security, UTM, aerospace and defence.

The platform spans six products — DroneDOOT™, DroneSENSE™, DroneYUG™, DroneARM™, DroneWALL™ / DIWAR™ and DroneDRISHTI™. Avgarde was founded in India as a private limited company, with offices in Bangalore and Guwahati and partnerships with IISc Bangalore and IIT Guwahati.
