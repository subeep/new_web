How it works

One integrated stack — from signal to decision.

A proprietary deep neural model for micro-Doppler signature classification, paired with a parallel algorithm for low-latency range-Doppler mapping. The result: actionable data, decisioning tools and countermeasures for drones, UAVs, birds and other aerial objects.

01

Sense

Active 3D radar and passive wide-band RF sensors on a Software Defined Radio architecture.

02

Process

FPGA waveform streaming, clutter reduction and motion-artifact cancellation.

03

Classify

Deep neural AI auto-classifies micro-Doppler signatures — drone, UAV, bird or clutter.

04

Respond

Soft-kill jamming, GNSS spoofing and safe-landing countermeasures.

Advantages

Six advantages built into the stack.

From the neural model to the radio architecture, each layer is engineered for range, accuracy and speed.

Deep neural detection

AI model enhances detection range by 30% compared to conventional systems.

Auto-classification

Automatic classification of aerial targets for effective management of Low Altitude Airspace (LAA).

Clutter cancellation

Clutter reduction plus SVM-based clutter cancellation improve target detection by 60%.

Motion artifact removal

A dedicated cancellation algorithm improves classification of radar signatures.

FPGA acceleration

High-throughput waveform streaming through an FPGA reduces time-to-compute by 35%.

SDR architecture

Software Defined Radio for scalability, modularity and reconfigurability — with an easy, operator-friendly UI.
