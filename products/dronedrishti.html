Overview

What DroneDRISHTI™ does.

An ultra-lightweight, drone-mountable ‘Identification of Friend or Foe (IFF)’ remote ID broadcaster module providing vital information such as location coordinates, accelerometer and gyroscopic data.

It can be integrated with Avgarde’s drone detection systems to categorise between white-listed and black-listed drones.
