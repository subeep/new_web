Overview

What DroneYUG™ does.

A soft-kill electronic warfare (EW) system specifically designed for countering drone threats through an orchestration of jamming and spoofing mechanisms. The system features a proprietary jamming mechanism to tackle FHSS drones and provides a long jamming range.

It has an integrated GPS spoofing unit that can take over the GPS system of the drones and gives the user manoeuvring capabilities for bringing the drone to a safe landing position for forensic and other analysis. It can be configured in a wake-and-sleep model and can be integrated with RF / EO-IR / camera-based detection systems.
