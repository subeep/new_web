Overview

What DroneSENSE™ does.

A passive drone detection system that leverages emitted radio signals from drones to remote controllers for the purpose of telemetry and video streaming. It incorporates multi-layer inference engines (MLIE) to detect the presence of drones in any given area.

Due to the passive technology of the system, negligible probability of intercept can be achieved. Additionally, the solution is protocol agnostic as it can detect any make or model of drones. DroneSENSE™ can be integrated with multiple dual-use platforms.
