Market detail

Challenges and outcomes.

Aircraft Safety & Airport Operators

With increased drone usage, the low-altitude airspace is converging with conventional aviation. Stakeholders are rethinking traditional air traffic control to mitigate drone risks in restricted areas.

  • 360° aerial situational awareness from drones, UAVs and aerial objects
  • Enhanced safety for aircraft, passengers and airport assets
  • Reduced revenue losses from airport shutdowns due to drone intrusions

Critical Infrastructure Protection

Over 70% of global critical infrastructure is unprotected from aerial risks — most perimeter intrusion detection systems (PIDS) don’t cover the low-altitude airspace. Utilities, oil, gas & energy, strategic assets and high-value industries all face this gap.

  • Enforce ‘no drone zones’ with multi-layered PIDS
  • Minimise interruptions to site operations from drone incursions
  • Prevent drone-related incidents through countermeasures and operator deterrence

UAV Traffic Management

Managing unmanned traffic is critical to the rapid expansion of commercial drone operations and advanced aerial mobility. UTM frameworks are being adopted by fleet operators, integrators and logistics networks worldwide.

  • A platform approach combining AI/ML, sensor fusion and emerging technologies
  • Airspace intelligence — situational awareness, topographical data, fleet waypoints and optimal paths
  • Precise real-time tracking of large numbers of drones

Military & Security Agencies

With drones, perimeter defence now extends into the airspace. Modular, portable, reconfigurable and interoperable solutions address rogue drones across army, air force, navy, border and homeland security.

  • Detect, classify, mitigate and report threats — 360° coverage, 24/7 scan and tracking
  • Exceptional range resolution, micro-target detection and GNSS / WiFi / telemetry jamming
  • AI-based target classification to support ISR strategies
  • Soft-kill EW countermeasures via proprietary jamming and spoofing
